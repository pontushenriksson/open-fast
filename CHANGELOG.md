# Changelog

All notable changes to Open Fast are documented here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project uses [semantic versioning](https://semver.org).

## [1.0.0] – 2026-10-09

First release.

### Added

- Fasting timer with a progress dial, time left, percentage and overtime.
- Start a fast after the fact, edit the start time during a fast and the end time when saving.
- 12 fasting methods (12:12 to 72 h, 5:2, Eat-Stop-Eat, ADF) plus a custom goal.
- 9 body phases from digestion to prolonged fasting, each with a "read more" sheet: what happens, main fuel, how it may feel, tips, evidence level and sources.
- "Does it break my fast?" with 39 foods, drinks and supplements, searchable in English and Swedish.
- Energy and weight estimates: energy used during a fast, fat equivalent, typical scale change and an expected-loss calculator, personalized with an optional profile.
- Progress: streaks, averages, goal rate, hours per day (7/30 days), weight log and editable history with undo.
- 19 verified research highlights with links to the original papers.
- Tips, do's and don'ts, how to break a fast, FAQ and safety information.
- English and Swedish, metric and imperial units, light and dark theme.
- Goal notifications (scheduled natively in the iOS/Android apps) and calendar reminders.
- Installable, offline-capable PWA; native iOS/Android builds through Capacitor.
- JSON export and import. All data stays on the device.
